⚠️ Important — trial version & data warning

Monetra is currently a development/trial product. It has not yet been fully launched as a public production service. The current version is a website under development and testing.

At the current stage:

  • The service is web-only.
  • The backend uses MySQL.
  • The current MySQL database is operated on development infrastructure, currently hosted on the developer's MacBook.
  • The final production infrastructure has not yet been finalized.
  • The security, backup, availability and recovery architecture may change before the public production launch.
  • Data may be migrated, reset, modified or deleted during development.

Please be careful with your data.

Do not enter

  • Bank passwords or online-banking login credentials
  • Bank PINs or card PINs
  • OTPs or 2FA codes
  • CVV/CVC codes
  • Recovery codes
  • Banking API keys
  • Other authentication credentials

Monetra is designed to track information such as account balances, transactions, income, expenses, investments, debts, budgets and financial plans. You should only provide information that you are comfortable entering into the current trial version.

By using Monetra during this trial phase, you acknowledge that you are using a development-stage service and understand the current limitations of its infrastructure. This acknowledgement does not remove or reduce any rights you have under applicable data-protection law.

2. What Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you use Monetra.

2.1 Account information — name, username, email address, phone number, profile picture, password-related authentication information, account creation date, verification status.

3. Financial Information

Monetra may process financial information that you voluntarily enter. This can include:

Bank and account information — bank name, account name, account type, currency, balance.

Transactions — date, transaction type, account, category, amount, currency, payment method, notes.

Monthly planning — expected income, expected expenses, budget amounts, planned payments, expected savings, spending limits.

Investments — stock name, ticker, exchange, quantity, purchase price, current value, portfolio information.

Debts — debt name, debt amount, remaining balance, EMI, due date, payment status.

This information may be considered sensitive in context, even where it does not constitute a special category of personal data under GDPR. We treat financial information as confidential and use it only for legitimate purposes connected to providing and operating Monetra.

4. Information We Do NOT Want You to Provide

You must not provide banking passwords, bank PINs, card PINs, OTPs, authentication codes, CVV/CVC codes, recovery codes, banking API keys, security questions and answers, or other credentials used to authenticate you to a financial institution. Monetra does not need these credentials to provide its manual financial-tracking functionality.

5. Technical Information

Depending on the technical implementation, Monetra may process IP address, browser information, device information, operating system, login timestamps, session information, error logs, security logs, and technical diagnostics. We use such information where necessary for security, authentication, troubleshooting, service operation, and preventing abuse.

6. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to create and authenticate your account, verify your email or phone number, store your financial information, display your accounts, record transactions, calculate net worth, track investments and their performance, convert currencies, track debts and EMI payments, create monthly plans, calculate planned versus actual spending, generate financial charts, provide support, protect the service, detect abuse, fix technical problems, improve Monetra, and comply with legal obligations.

7. Legal Basis for Processing

Where GDPR applies, personal-data processing must have an applicable legal basis. GDPR Article 6 includes bases such as consent, contractual necessity, legal obligations and legitimate interests. Depending on the specific processing activity, Monetra may rely on:

Contractual necessity — processing necessary to provide the Monetra service you request.

Consent — where we specifically ask for your consent and applicable law requires consent.

Legitimate interests — for purposes such as security, preventing abuse, protecting the service, troubleshooting, and improving service reliability, where the relevant legal requirements for legitimate interests are satisfied.

Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

The specific legal basis depends on the processing activity.

8. Your Financial Data

Your financial data is used to provide Monetra's financial-tracking features — for example, connecting accounts, transactions, investments and debts to calculate net worth. Your financial information is not used to provide personalized investment advice. We do not sell your personal financial information.

9. Stock Market Data

When you add an investment, Monetra may need to identify the relevant stock in order to retrieve its current or latest available market price — for example, using the stock ticker, exchange, or market identifier to obtain market information from a third-party provider. Your complete financial profile does not need to be provided to a market-data provider merely to retrieve a stock price.

10. Currency Exchange Data

Monetra may obtain exchange-rate information from third-party providers, used to convert currencies (for example EUR to INR), calculate portfolio values, calculate total net worth, and display financial summaries in your selected currency. Exchange rates may be delayed or unavailable.

11. Third-Party Providers

Monetra may use third-party providers for market data, exchange rates, hosting, email, authentication, security, database infrastructure, technical monitoring, and other necessary services. The actual providers used by Monetra will depend on the final technical architecture. Where applicable, third-party processors will be subject to appropriate contractual and legal requirements.

12. Current Development Infrastructure

During the current trial phase, Monetra's database is operated using MySQL on development infrastructure currently hosted on the developer's MacBook. This is not necessarily the infrastructure that will be used for the final production release. The infrastructure may later be migrated to professionally managed production hosting. If this happens, this Privacy Policy will be updated where necessary to accurately describe the new processing environment.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. Depending on the current technical implementation, security measures may include authentication controls, password hashing, access controls, database permissions, encryption in transit, secure session management, security monitoring, backups where implemented, and restricted administrative access.

However, because Monetra is currently a trial/development product, we cannot represent that the current environment provides the same security, redundancy or resilience expected of a mature production financial platform. Users should therefore exercise caution and should not enter highly sensitive authentication information. GDPR Article 32 requires controllers and processors to implement security measures appropriate to the risk, taking into account factors such as the nature, scope and context of processing.

14. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required by law. For an active account, information may generally be retained so that Monetra can provide the requested financial-tracking functionality.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize applicable information within a reasonable period, subject to legal obligations, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, backup limitations, and other lawful retention requirements. Where a specific retention period cannot reasonably be stated, we use criteria such as the purpose of processing, account status and applicable legal requirements. GDPR Article 13 requires disclosure of the retention period or, where that cannot be specified, the criteria used to determine it.
