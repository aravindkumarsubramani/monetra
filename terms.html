1. About Monetra

Monetra is a personal finance tracking platform designed to help users organize and understand their financial information. Monetra may allow you to:

  • Add financial accounts, bank accounts, cards and wallets
  • Track balances across multiple currencies
  • Record income, expenses and transfers
  • Create monthly budgets and plan monthly payments
  • Track debts and EMI payments
  • Track investment holdings, including Indian and international stocks
  • View current or latest available stock prices and currency exchange rates
  • Calculate investment values, profit and loss, and net worth
  • View financial charts and summaries

6. Net Worth Calculations

Monetra may calculate your estimated net worth based on information available in your account. A simplified calculation may be:

Net Worth = Assets − Liabilities

Assets may include bank balances, cash/wallet balances, investment values, and other assets entered by the user.

Liabilities may include debts, loans, and EMI-related outstanding amounts.

Net-worth calculations are provided for personal tracking purposes. They should not be treated as audited financial statements.
