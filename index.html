What you get

Everything for tracking your money, in one app

From daily transactions to long-term net worth — six focused tools that all update the same dashboard.

One dashboard for everything

Net worth, total assets and debts, this month's cash flow, and a 12-month savings trend — all in the currency you choose.

Accounts, cards & wallets

Every bank account, cash wallet, and card in one place — each with its own currency, converted automatically for your totals.

Transactions, made fast

Log pay, receive, transfer, withdraw or deposit inline — no extra clicks — with reusable templates for the ones you repeat.

Monthly planner

Budget by category, track recurring bills with due dates, and see your expected cash flow before the month happens.

Debts, PayLater & investments

Watch payoff progress, BNPL balances, and your Indian & international stock portfolio's performance, side by side.

Private, local-first, multi-currency

No server, no syncing — everything is saved to this browser only, and converts between INR, EUR, USD and more.
